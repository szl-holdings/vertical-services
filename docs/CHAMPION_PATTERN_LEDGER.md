# Champion Pattern Ledger — Vertical Frontier Fabric v1

## Purpose

This ledger converts publicly observable product patterns into original SZL
interaction principles. It is not a cloning specification. It authorizes neither
copying proprietary code nor reproducing another company's distinctive trade
dress, content, datasets, illustrations, motion assets, or private interfaces.

The intake rule is:

> Observe the problem-solving pattern, cite the public source, express the
> principle in neutral language, then implement a distinct SZL interaction and
> evidence model from first principles.

## Vertical category leaders and the gap SZL targets

### A11oy — governed decision infrastructure

The category contains AI assistants, workflow automation, data platforms, and
ontology products. The missing product object is usually the complete decision
trajectory:

`signal → proposal → policy → human bind → bounded effect → receipt → verification`

A11oy makes that trajectory inspectable and refuses to let the proposing model
mint its own authority.

### Hatun — executive cognition

Knowledge bases remember documents. Task systems remember assignments. Meeting
assistants remember transcripts. Hatun's lane is the temporal strategic memory
between them: why a decision changed, which commitment followed, who owns it,
and whether the expected outcome occurred.

### Killinchu — defense and maritime intelligence

The product differentiator is not another tactical map. It is an uncertainty-
and-policy-aware track object with source quality, simulation alternatives,
human review, and explicit separation between public demonstration and any
operator-owned effect path.

### Sentra — cyber risk and response

Sentra prioritizes reachable business consequence rather than accumulating
findings. An exposure is useful only when the system can show the exploited
signal, asset identity, attack path, control state, response decision, and
remediation evidence.

### Lyte — business observability

Lyte extends observability beyond telemetry correlation. The product must show
what changed, which users or business outcomes were affected, what action was
taken, and whether the action improved the measured outcome.

### PURIQ Finance — evidence-backed market research

PURIQ is not a trading oracle. It creates versioned filing evidence, assumption
sets, scenario ranges, counter-evidence, catalysts, and invalidation conditions.
The human investment decision remains outside the model.

### Terra — parcel and civic change intelligence

Terra joins parcel facts through time. Its original object is a parcel twin that
shows ownership lineage, zoning and regulatory deltas, risk layers, assumptions,
and the decision record instead of only a map or valuation output.

### PRISM Counsel — matter and argument command

PRISM keeps record facts, allegations, authority, inference, counterargument,
deadline, reviewer, and approved work product distinct. This separation is the
basis for reviewability and controlled legal drafting.

### Living Anatomy — system-body intelligence

Living Anatomy turns architecture into a stateful body. Users can inspect organ
health, dependency circulation, bounded failure injection, recovery, and proof
without treating a visual effect as production evidence.

### SZL Atlas — public estate explorer

Atlas provides one truthful public map of artifacts, source, runtime state, and
limitations. It is discovery infrastructure, not a substitute for each
artifact's own evaluation and lineage contract.

## Non-negotiable intake boundary

Allowed inputs:

- public product documentation and public user interfaces as research;
- official public APIs and public-domain government data under their terms;
- permissively licensed open-source components with license, revision, and
  modification provenance;
- operator-owned data supplied through an explicit connector contract.

Prohibited inputs:

- proprietary source code, leaked repositories, credentials, or private APIs;
- scraping that violates access controls, robots directives, contractual terms,
  or rate limits;
- copying distinctive layouts, illustrations, brand language, or motion assets;
- relabeling another team's model, benchmark, dataset, or research as SZL work;
- treating an HTTP response, download count, or polished interface as proof of
  capability, safety, adoption, or authorization.
