## Vertical champion map

### A11oy — governed AI command fabric

Public leaders and adjacent systems:

- [Palantir AIP](https://www.palantir.com/platforms/aip/)
- [IBM watsonx.governance](https://www.ibm.com/products/watsonx-governance)
- [Microsoft Responsible AI](https://www.microsoft.com/ai/responsible-ai)
- [Credo AI](https://www.credo.ai/)

Patterns to retain:

- model operations attached to business objects rather than isolated prompts;
- policy and approval surfaced before consequential action;
- evaluations, monitoring, and governance treated as continuous operations;
- clear separation between recommendation and execution.

SZL lane competitors usually leave open:

> A decision compiler that preserves proposal, evidence, policy, human binding,
> execution boundary, outcome, and verification as one reconstructable chain.

### Killinchu — defense and maritime decision intelligence

Public leaders and adjacent systems:

- [Anduril Lattice](https://www.anduril.com/lattice/)
- [Palantir Defense](https://www.palantir.com/offerings/defense/)
- [True Anomaly](https://www.trueanomaly.space/)
- [Windward](https://windward.ai/)
- [Spire Maritime](https://spire.com/maritime/)
- [Kpler Maritime](https://www.kpler.com/solutions/maritime)

Patterns to retain:

- map-first situational context;
- multi-source track fusion and anomaly explanation;
- operator-centered mission workflows;
- explicit source age, confidence, and simulation state.

SZL lane competitors usually leave open:

> A single evidence chain joining maritime anomaly, ownership and sanctions
> context, mission simulation, operator recommendation, and after-action receipt,
> while keeping public physical actuation disabled.

### Lyte — business observability and execution intelligence

Public leaders and adjacent systems:

- [Datadog](https://www.datadoghq.com/)
- [New Relic](https://newrelic.com/platform)
- [Dynatrace](https://www.dynatrace.com/platform/)
- [Grafana](https://grafana.com/)
- [Honeycomb](https://www.honeycomb.io/)
- [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/)

Patterns to retain:

- traces, metrics, logs, profiles, cost, and user impact in one investigation;
- topology over disconnected charts;
- fast drill-down from symptom to service and owner;
- open telemetry contracts and portable instrumentation.

SZL lane competitors usually leave open:

> An outcome graph that explains which technical signal threatens which customer,
> revenue, delivery, or governance commitment; names the owner and decision due;
> and later verifies the business outcome.

### Sentra — cyber exposure and remediation command

Public leaders and adjacent systems:

- [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/)
- [CrowdStrike Falcon](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/platform/)
- [Palo Alto Cortex](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/cortex)
- [Microsoft Defender](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/microsoft-defender)
- [CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

Patterns to retain:

- attack-path context instead of vulnerability counts alone;
- exploitability, identity, data, control, and criticality joined in a graph;
- remediation ownership and proof of closure;
- official exploitation evidence separated from local asset exposure.

SZL lane competitors usually leave open:

> A remediation compiler that joins known exploitation, asset criticality,
> attack path, compensating control, owner capacity, proposed change, approval,
> and closure evidence without exposing an exploit path to the public product.

### Terra — property and place intelligence

Public leaders and adjacent systems:

- [Esri ArcGIS](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/index)
- [Regrid](https://regrid.com/)
- [MapLibre](https://maplibre.org/)
- [FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer](https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/national-flood-hazard-layer)
- [NYC PLUTO](https://www.nyc.gov/site/planning/data-maps/open-data/dwn-pluto-mappluto.page)

Patterns to retain:

- map and parcel as the primary object;
- layer-by-layer public-record and hazard context;
- clear distinction between observed record, estimate, and scenario;
- exportable diligence packets and provenance.

SZL lane competitors usually leave open:

> A parcel twin that explains why a risk or opportunity changed, which evidence
> supports it, what remains unresolved, and which human decision is next—without
> becoming an appraisal, lending engine, or protected-class targeting system.

### PURIQ Finance — evidence-first financial research

### Living Anatomy — system health and governed cognition

Public leaders and adjacent systems:

- [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/)
- [Backstage](https://backstage.io/)
- [Grafana](https://grafana.com/)
- [CNCF OpenTelemetry project](https://github.com/open-telemetry)

Patterns to retain:

- common telemetry vocabulary;
- dependency and ownership maps;

> risk; and records why the body held or failed closed.

## Open-source adoption lanes

The current implementation is deliberately dependency-free. Future components
may be adopted only through an exact revision and license review.

| Candidate | Potential role | Adoption rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenTelemetry](https://github.com/open-telemetry) | portable traces, metrics, and logs | pin exact SDK revision; preserve Apache-2.0 notices; keep operator data private |
| [MapLibre GL JS](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js) | Terra and Killinchu geospatial rendering | verify exact license and transitive assets at pin time; no vendor basemap assumptions |
| [deck.gl](https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl) | large geospatial layers | verify exact license and WebGL dependency closure |
| [Cytoscape.js](https://github.com/cytoscape/cytoscape.js) | Sentra and Lyte graph exploration | verify exact MIT license and accessibility behavior |
| [Apache ECharts](https://github.com/apache/echarts) | dense evidence and research visualization | preserve Apache notices and verify bundle provenance |
| [DuckDB](https://github.com/duckdb/duckdb) | local analytical evidence processing | pin engine and extensions; prohibit unreviewed remote extension loading |
| [pgvector](https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector) | operator-owned evidence retrieval | keep tenant isolation and source citations outside model memory |
| [FastAPI](https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi) | future typed service decomposition | pin full dependency closure before replacing the stdlib reference runtime |
| [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm) | operator-hosted OpenAI-compatible model serving | model license, weights, GPU image, endpoint identity, and cost cap must be bound |
| [KServe](https://github.com/kserve/kserve) | governed model deployment | use only in an operator-owned cluster with signed images and admission policy |

`VERIFY_AT_PIN_TIME` is mandatory. Repository visibility, popularity, or an old
license memory is not sufficient authorization to incorporate code.

## Official-data rule

Official public data is used through documented public interfaces and within its
terms, rate limits, attribution, privacy, and retention boundaries. “Publicly
reachable” is not equivalent to unrestricted copying, redistribution, or silent
commercial reuse. Private, leaked, bypassed, or credential-gated data is outside
this build.

## Design conclusion

The differentiator is not a collage of leader aesthetics. It is the combination
leaders rarely expose in one place:

```text
vertical-specific operating instrument
+ official-source freshness
+ governed model role
+ portable kernel trace
+ bounded memory
+ explicit human authority
+ deterministic receipt
+ cross-vertical outcome graph
```

That combination is the shared SZL moat. Each vertical must remain unmistakably
itself while contributing evidence, operating knowledge, and reusable kernels to
the wider command fabric.
